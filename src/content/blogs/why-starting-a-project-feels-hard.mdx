# Why Starting a Project Feels Hard

Once, I tried to pick up a tool just by thinking about moving my hand, without actually moving it. No matter how hard I tried, nothing happened. 

It terrified me. It made me ask a simple question: **what actually moves my hand?**

I realized that thinking and doing are two completely different things:

* **Thinking** happens consciously.
* **Doing** happens automatically.

Isn't it amazing that the most important things we do happen without overthinking?

When you want to start a project, stop thinking about doing it. Just make the first physical movement—open the document, pick up the pen, or write the first line of code—and let execution follow.

> "The way to get started is to quit talking and begin doing."  
> — **Walt Disney**
